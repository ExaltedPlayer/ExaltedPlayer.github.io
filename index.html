About

Tutoring by students who just took the test

A&S SAT was started by two recent high school graduates who recently scored in the 98th percentile on the digital SAT. We know the current test inside and out.

Our approach is diagnostic-driven. Every student starts with a full diagnostic session so we know exactly where their points are being lost. Sessions are then built around those weak areas, not a generic curriculum that wastes time on things they already know.

We keep classes small and private lessons personalized. Sessions are continually tailored to the students need by hand grading student homework and finding weak points.

Curriculum

What each week looks like

Every curriculum starts with a diagnostic. Weak areas identified in session one determine where the rest of the time goes.

Week Focus Session Content
Week 1DiagnosticFull timed diagnostic. Score breakdown by section and sub-skill. Identify top 3 weak areas. Set a realistic target score.
Week 2Weak area #1Deep dive into the primary weak area. Concept teaching, worked examples, 20-min timed practice set, and review.
Week 3Weak area #2Same format — concept teaching, worked examples, timed practice. Compare to Week 2 performance and track improvement.
Week 4Weak area #3Address the third weak area. Begin connecting all three — students often make the same conceptual error across sections.
Week 5Cross-section strategyPacing, guessing rules, question-order strategy, elimination techniques. Applicable across both Math and Reading & Writing.
Week 6Targeted practiceMixed practice set from all three weak areas. Identify what has improved and what still needs work going into the final stretch.
Week 7Test simulation35 minutes of timed full-section practice (Math or R&W, whichever is weaker), followed by 25 minutes of immediate error review.
Week 8Final reviewReview remaining error patterns by category. Final reinforcement of weakest area. Mental prep and strategy for test day.
Week Focus Session Content
Week 1DiagnosticTimed diagnostic — one full section (Math or R&W based on student priority). Score breakdown, identify top 2 weak areas, set target.
Week 2Weak areasAddress both weak areas in one session — 25 minutes each. Focus on highest-impact concepts first. Homework assigned.
Week 3Strategy + practicePacing, elimination, and guessing rules. Mixed timed practice from both weak areas. Review homework from Week 2.
Week 4Final pushTimed section practice followed by error review by category. Final reinforcement of weakest remaining area.
