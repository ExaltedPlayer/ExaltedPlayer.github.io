Curriculum

How tutoring is structured

Every curriculum starts with a diagnostic. Weak areas identified in the diagnostic determine what areas are focused on.

Private tutoring begins with a diagnostic to identify each student's strengths and weaknesses. In the first session, the tutor reviews every missed question — explaining both how to solve it and why the student missed it — and categorizes each mistake as a content gap, timing issue, careless error, or strategy problem.

From there, the tutor builds a personalized lesson plan focused on the student's highest-priority areas, whether that's Algebra, Advanced Math, Grammar, Reading Comprehension, or pacing. Targeted homework is assigned after each session and reviewed at the start of the next before moving on.

As the test date approaches, sessions shift toward mixed practice, timed sections, Desmos strategies, and full practice test review. The structure stays flexible — focused at every stage on the areas that will improve the score fastest.
